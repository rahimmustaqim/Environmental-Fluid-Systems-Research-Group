Sustainable Living Environment

Building a safer and more sustainable environment through science-based engineering.

EFSRG supports water, energy, climate, and environmental infrastructure through research-based modelling and technical analysis.

Our expertise is aligned with selected Sustainable Development Goals by supporting clean water systems, renewable energy development, resilient infrastructure, safer communities, and climate adaptation.
