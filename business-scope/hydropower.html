Why this matters

Hydropower Support and Water Infrastructure Assessment

Hydropower development requires a strong understanding of water availability, river flow behaviour, sediment movement, intake performance, and environmental conditions. Without proper technical assessment, hydropower facilities may face reduced energy production, sedimentation problems, operational instability, or insufficient water supply during dry periods.

Main purpose: to support hydropower planning, feasibility assessment, operational evaluation, and infrastructure design through hydrological, hydraulic, sediment, and environmental analysis.

Key Questions Addressed

  • Is the river flow sufficient to support hydropower generation throughout the year?
  • How do seasonal rainfall and dry-period conditions affect water availability?
  • Where are sedimentation, erosion, or intake blockage risks most likely to occur?
  • How will the proposed structure affect flow distribution, water levels, and downstream conditions?
  • What operational or design improvements can increase performance and reduce risk?

Typical Analysis

Hydrological Assessment

Rainfall analysis, river discharge estimation, flow duration curve development, water availability assessment, and seasonal flow evaluation.

Hydraulic Modelling

River flow simulation, water level analysis, intake performance assessment, diversion channel evaluation, and structure-related flow impact analysis.

Sediment Risk Evaluation

Assessment of sediment transport, deposition risk, erosion potential, intake sedimentation, and reservoir or channel maintenance needs.

Operational Support

Evaluation of flow regulation, design discharge, operational scenarios, and technical recommendations for safer and more reliable hydropower performance.

Why It Is Important

Hydropower systems depend on stable and reliable water resources. Technical modelling helps determine whether the available river flow can meet energy production targets while considering seasonal variation, extreme rainfall, sedimentation, and environmental constraints.

The analysis is also important for evaluating the performance of intakes, diversion channels, spillways, reservoirs, and downstream river sections. It provides quantitative evidence to support design improvement, operational planning, and risk reduction.

Expected Outputs

  • Water availability and flow duration analysis.
  • Design discharge and seasonal river flow assessment.
  • Hydraulic modelling of river, intake, channel, or related structures.
  • Sedimentation and erosion risk evaluation.
  • Comparison of existing and proposed design or operational scenarios.
  • Technical recommendations for hydropower planning and performance improvement.

Application Areas

This service can be applied to hydropower feasibility studies, mini-hydro and micro-hydro planning, pumped-storage support, intake and diversion design, sedimentation risk assessment, river infrastructure evaluation, and renewable energy development projects.
